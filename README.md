# Adidas-Sales-Analysis
An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing $900M in Adidas US sales across 2020–2021. It delivers actionable business intelligence on revenue drivers, a 42% operating margin, regional demand, and retail partner performance to optimize inventory allocation and wholesale strategy.
## Important Tools & Technologies

-Power BI Desktop: Used as the core platform for end-to-end data transformation, visualization, and dashboard authoring.

-Power Query (M Language): Employed for Extract, Transform, Load (ETL) processes—handling data cleansing, formatting types, date parsing, and removing nulls.

-DAX (Data Analysis Expressions): Utilized for dynamic aggregations, margin ratios, and time-based metrics.

-Data Modeling: Star schema architecture establishing relationships between transactional sales facts and dimension tables (Date, Geography, Product, Retailer).

## Power BI Native Visuals:

-Choropleth Filled Map: Spatial sales distribution across US states.

-Area / Line Chart: Monthly seasonality and demand trend trajectory.

-Donut Chart: Proportional market share by geographic region.

-Horizontal Bar Charts: Ranked breakdown for product lines and distribution retailers.

-Interactive Slicers: Date range picker and regional dropdown selectors.

# Conclusion

Adidas achieved strong commercial efficiency with a 42% operating margin ($332M profit on $900M revenue), primarily anchored by the West region (30% share) and Men's Street Footwear ($209M). Strategic growth depends on prioritizing high-yield wholesale partners like West Gear and Foot Locker, expanding market penetration in underperforming regions like the Midwest (15%), and aligning supply chain inventory with peak seasonal demand surges in July–August and December.
